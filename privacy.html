Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MyJar handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective: July 27, 2026Last updated: July 27, 2026

The short version

Your savings goals and transaction history stay on your device. MyJar has no account system, advertising, or analytics. Optional online features contact weather and exchange-rate providers only for the feature you request.

01

Who we are

This policy applies to MyJar, package name com.myjar.app.

Privacy questions: sandipvai4456@gmail.com.

02

Information stored on your device

MyJar stores jar names, target and saved amounts, deadlines, status, deposits, withdrawals, and app preferences locally. Cached exchange rates and optional weather display data may also be stored locally.

This information is not uploaded to a MyJar account or server because MyJar has no account or cloud-storage service.

03

Approximate location and weather

If you enable Location Weather, MyJar requests approximate foreground location. Coordinates rounded to two decimal places are sent to OpenWeatherMap to retrieve current weather for the decorative app window.

MyJar does not request background location, create a location history, use location for advertising, or sell location data. You can decline or revoke permission at any time.

04

Exchange rates

MyJar requests public currency rates from ExchangeRate-API for converted totals. Requests do not include jar names, savings amounts, or transaction history. External services may receive normal network data such as IP address and request time.

05

Biometric authentication

If biometric lock is enabled, Android performs authentication. MyJar receives only the result and does not receive, store, or transmit biometric templates.

06

Notifications

MyJar may request permission for optional local reminders where available. Scheduling happens on your device. MyJar does not use notification advertising or send savings details to a notification server.

07

Third-party services

MyJar does not sell personal information and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

OpenWeatherMapApproximate coordinates for optional weather.
ExchangeRate-APIPublic currency rates; no savings details.
Android servicesPermissions, biometrics, local notifications, and file saving.
08

Security

Core savings information stays in private local app storage. Online requests use HTTPS. Optional biometric lock adds device-level protection. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

09

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it. Use Settings → Your data → Clear all MyJar data, or uninstall MyJar. Android backup or device-management settings may operate outside MyJar’s control.

MyJar has no cloud account data to delete.

10

Children’s privacy

MyJar is a general-audience savings utility and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will show a new “Last updated” date.

12

Contact
