Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MyJar handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective: November 26, 2026Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

MyJar keeps your savings goals on your device and also backs them up to the cloud, so you don't lose your progress if you change or reset your phone. To do that, MyJar creates an anonymous account for each installation. You can link it to your Google account if you want to restore your progress on another device.

MyJar also records simple usage counts, such as how often the app is opened and how many deposits are made, so we can see which features help people save. Those counts never include jar names, notes, or amounts.

MyJar shows no advertising, does not sell your information, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It uses Firebase (Google) for accounts, backup, usage counts, analytics, and crash reports, and RevenueCat to check whether MyJar Pro is active.

02

Information stored on your device

MyJar stores jar names, target and saved amounts, deadlines, notes, status, deposits, withdrawals, garden progress, and app preferences locally on your device. Cached exchange rates and optional weather display data are also stored locally and stay on the device.

The savings and garden information listed above is also backed up to the cloud, as described in section 04.

03

Accounts and sign-in

Automatic anonymous account. When you first use MyJar, the app creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication. This account has a randomly generated user ID and does not include your name, email address, or phone number. MyJar uses it to keep your backup and usage counts together and to protect them, so that only your installation can read or change them.

Optional Google sign-in. If you choose Settings → Account & backup → Sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account ID with Firebase Authentication, and your anonymous account becomes linked to your Google account. You can then sign in on another device and restore your progress. MyJar does not request access to your contacts, Gmail, Drive, or any other Google data.

MyJar uses your name or email address only to show which account you are signed in with. They are not copied into the backup or usage records described below.

04

Cloud backup

MyJar backs up your progress to Google Cloud Firestore, a database service run by Google as part of Firebase. The backup is linked to your account's user ID and contains:

  • your jars: names, target and saved amounts, currencies, deadlines, notes, colors, and status;
  • your deposit and withdrawal history, including amounts, dates, and notes;
  • your app preferences stored with your jars, such as theme, language, and date format;
  • your garden progress, and which garden tips you have already seen.

MyJar makes a backup automatically when you leave the app, if anything has changed since the last one. You can also back up or restore manually in Settings → Account & backup. Each backup replaces the previous one, so MyJar keeps only your latest backup.

Backups do not include your garden trial status, cached exchange rates, weather data, notification settings on your device, biometric data, or payment details.

Security rules on the database allow only your own signed-in account to read, change, or delete your backup. The developer can access the database to operate the service, but does not read individual backups except to help you when you ask, or when required by law.

05

Usage statistics

To understand how MyJar is used and which features to improve, MyJar stores a small usage record for each account in Cloud Firestore. It contains:

  • an account summary: sign-in type (anonymous or Google), platform, app version, device language, whether MyJar Pro is active, the number of jars (total, active, and completed), the number of saved entries, garden level, and when the account was first and last seen;
  • daily counts: how many times the app was opened, deposits and withdrawals made, jars created and completed, garden visits, backups, restores, and Google sign-ins on that day.

These records contain no jar names, notes, amounts, currencies, deadlines, or transaction details. They are linked to your account's random user ID, not to your name or email address. The developer views them in a private dashboard, mostly as totals across all users.

06

Usage analytics

MyJar also uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate, for example which screens people open and whether a release is being adopted.

MyJar records screen views, plus a "login" event, with no other details, when you sign in with Google. It does not send Firebase Analytics any event containing your savings information: no jar names, amounts, deadlines, or transaction history.

Firebase Analytics automatically collects some technical information of its own, including a randomly generated app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, session and first-open timestamps, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. This identifier is specific to this app on this device.

MyJar contains no advertising SDK, does not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA or Android Advertising ID), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

07

Crash reporting

MyJar uses Firebase Crashlytics so that crashes and unhandled errors can be found and fixed. When the app fails, a report is sent containing the error and stack trace, the device model and operating system version, the app version, and general device state such as available memory.

Crash reports describe the code that failed. They are not designed to carry your savings data, and MyJar does not attach jar names or amounts to them.

08

Push notifications

MyJar uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional announcements, such as news about a new feature. To deliver these, Apple or Google issues your device a registration token, which Firebase holds.

A token identifies a device installation, not you. MyJar does not attach your savings information to it and does not send savings details through a notification server. Push messages are separate from savings reminders, which are described in section 10.

09

Purchases and MyJar Pro

MyJar Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details entirely, and MyJar never sees or stores your card number.

MyJar uses RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and transaction information from the store, the store country, and basic device information, and issues a randomly generated anonymous identifier for the installation.

MyJar does not send RevenueCat your name, email address, or any savings information. Whether Pro is active is recorded in the usage summary described in section 05.

10

Savings reminders

MyJar may request permission for optional reminders. Your device's operating system schedules and shows these entirely on the device. Their contents are not sent anywhere.

11

Approximate location and weather

If you enable Location Weather, MyJar requests approximate foreground location. Coordinates rounded to two decimal places are sent to OpenWeatherMap to retrieve current weather for the decorative app window.

MyJar does not request background location, create a location history, store your location in its backup or usage records, use location for advertising, or sell location data. You can decline or revoke permission at any time.

12

Exchange rates

MyJar requests public currency rates from ExchangeRate-API for converted totals. Requests do not include jar names, savings amounts, or transaction history. External services may receive normal network data such as IP address and request time.

13

Biometric authentication

If biometric lock is enabled, your device performs the authentication: Face ID or Touch ID on iOS, or the biometric prompt on Android. MyJar receives only the result and does not receive, store, or transmit biometric templates.

17

Your choices

  • Google sign-in. Optional. Sign in or out under Settings → Account & backup. When you sign out, your jars stay on the device and MyJar starts a new anonymous account for that installation.
  • Backup and restore. Back up or restore manually under Settings → Account & backup.
  • Delete your account and cloud data. See section 18.
  • Location. Turn off Settings → Privacy → Location weather in MyJar, or revoke the permission in your device settings.
  • Notifications. Turn reminders off in MyJar, or revoke the notification permission in your device settings. Revoking it also stops announcements.
  • Biometric lock. Turn it off under Settings → Privacy in MyJar.
  • Local data. Use Settings → Your data → Clear all MyJar data, or uninstall the app. If you clear data while the app is still installed, the cleared state replaces your cloud backup the next time you leave the app.
  • Usage statistics, analytics, and crash reporting. MyJar does not currently offer an in-app switch for these. You can object by emailing us, and uninstalling the app stops all further collection.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Californian residents have the right to know about, delete, and correct personal information, and are not discriminated against for using these rights. MyJar does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To use any of these rights, contact sandipvai4456@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

18

Deleting your account and data

In the app. Go to Settings → Account & backup → Delete account. This permanently deletes your cloud backup, your usage statistics, and your MyJar account in Firebase Authentication. Your jars stay on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app.

If you never signed in. Your account is anonymous, so it holds no name or email address that we could use to find it. To delete it, sign in with Google first. This links the same account rather than creating a new one. Then choose Delete account.

By email. If you signed in with Google, you can also email sandipvai4456@gmail.com from that Google address with the subject "Delete my MyJar account". We will delete your account, backup, and usage statistics within 30 days and confirm by email.

After deletion, if you keep using MyJar on that device, the app starts a new anonymous account and a new backup. Uninstall the app if you don't want that. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data are not linked to your MyJar account and are deleted on Google's own schedule (section 20).

19

Security

Your local savings information is kept in private app storage. All online requests use HTTPS, and Google encrypts data stored in Cloud Firestore. Database security rules restrict each backup to the account that created it, and only the developer can view the usage statistics. Optional biometric lock adds device-level protection. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

20

Retention

On your device: until you clear MyJar's data or uninstall the app.

Cloud backup, usage statistics, and account: until you delete your account (section 18). Each new backup replaces the previous one. If you sign in with a Google account that already has MyJar data, for example from another phone, the device's anonymous backup is deleted and its usage record is marked as merged. You then choose which progress to keep.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: retained under Google's retention settings and deleted on their schedule. Purchase records: retained by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google for as long as their policies and tax obligations require.
