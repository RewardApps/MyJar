Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does MyJar connect to my bank?

No. MyJar is a manual savings tracker and does not connect to bank accounts.

Where is my savings data stored?

Your jars, amounts, preferences, and transaction history are stored locally on your device.

Can I erase my data?

Yes. Use “Clear all MyJar data” in Settings or uninstall the app.
